AIOS Hermes

AIOS Hermes is a private, single-user productivity assistant operated by Marko Novakovic for his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's recent Gmail messages and Google Calendar events to prepare a private morning brief and a daily priority list.
  • Creates email drafts for the owner to review; it does not send email on its own.
  • Reads and creates Google Drive, Docs and Sheets files used in the owner's own work; it is configured not to delete, trash or share files.
  • Reads contacts to resolve names in the brief.

All processing runs on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.
