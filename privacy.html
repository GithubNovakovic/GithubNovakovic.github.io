Privacy Policy — AIOS Hermes

Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. Who operates the app

AIOS Hermes ("the app") is a private, single-user assistant operated by Marko Novakovic ("the owner") for his own Google account. The only user is the owner. Contact: marko.novakovic@gmail.com.

2. Google user data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app may access:

  • Gmail — read messages and settings, create and modify drafts and labels.
  • Google Calendar — read events and create or update events at the owner's request.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — read files and create or edit documents and spreadsheets.
  • Google Contacts — read-only, to resolve names.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the owner: a private morning brief, a daily priority list, triage of incoming documents, and drafts for the owner to review. The app is configured to block sending email, deleting or trashing Drive files, sharing files and deleting calendar events; such actions are performed only by the owner.

4. Storage and security

The app runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally with access restricted to the owner's user account. Summaries the app writes are stored locally on that computer. To generate summaries, excerpts may be processed by the AI model provider configured by the owner (Anthropic) under that provider's terms.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to determine creditworthiness and not transferred to third parties, except as required to provide the features above (AI model processing) or by law.

6. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On revocation, the owner deletes the locally stored tokens; locally stored summaries are deleted at the owner's discretion.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
